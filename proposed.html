Five areas of work

The CGIAR Climate Action programme works through five areas of work. This Hub is the entry point to all five. Each has its own page carrying what the area does, who to contact, the datasets and tools it draws on, its current use cases and its evidence base.

1

Prioritization and Coordination of Climate Action

Accelerating Climate Action

Connects climate science, modelling and expertise from across CGIAR to support adaptation and mitigation planning, investment decisions and global climate agendas. This Hub is the vehicle for that work.

Goes to Use-case portfolio Programme overview Annual technical report 2025
2

Digital Advisories and Climate Risk Management

Adapt to Variability and Extremes

Develops and scales digital weather and climate advisories, early warning systems, anticipatory action, insurance and other risk management products, working through national meteorological agencies, extension systems and humanitarian organisations.

Goes to African Agriculture Adaptation Atlas ACASA AgWise South Asia Drought Monitoring Work with the data directly
3

Locally-Led Adaptation

Empower Locally Led Action

Addresses the institutional, financial, governance and information barriers that prevent locally led adaptation from happening in practice, and works to embed successful models in policies, institutions and financing systems.

Goes to MELIAF Adaptation Activator Gender, equity and social inclusion

Thinnest area on the site at present. Needs content from the area-of-work lead.

4

Low-Emission Transitions

Transition to Low-Emission Development

Helps governments, businesses and development partners design affordable, just and verifiable pathways to reduce greenhouse gas emissions from food, land and water systems, including the measurement systems that implementation depends on.

Goes to AgMRV Tier 2 livestock uncertainty iCLEANED
5

Finance and Policy for Scaling Solutions

Finance Climate Ambition

Develops climate risk analytics, adaptation and mitigation investment cases, and supports financing mechanisms that direct capital where it delivers most. This is the area of most direct interest to the primary user proposed in the concept note.

Goes to GCF Preparation Facility Climate Security Observatory Work with the data directly
