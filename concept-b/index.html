What is real here. All 51 catalogued items, their providers and their links. Every count, bar, matrix cell and completeness score is computed from those items at page load, never typed in. The query and all three filters run entirely in your browser, with no network calls and nothing sent anywhere. Metadata for CHIRPS, ERA5, AgERA5 and the two Data Hub datasets was read from each provider's own dataset page on 10/08/2026. What is not real: the catalogue is 51 hand-entered items, not CGSpace or Gardian.

Evidence pack for the current query

The pack lists real sources, and states where it runs out. Each section is filled from the selection above, with provider, resolution and coverage against every citation, so it can be checked. It does not write prose and it asserts no figures: no country-level figures have been compiled, and inventing plausible ones would make this impossible to review honestly. The gaps in amber are the real limits of the catalogue, not placeholders.

Concept B of five design interpretations prepared on 10/08/2026 to elicit feedback on the structure of the CGIAR Climate Hub. Built as a console because its proposition is that you arrive with a question and want the evidence assessed, not browsed. Nothing here is an approved CGIAR position.
