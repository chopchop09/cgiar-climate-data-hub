Experts

Find a climate expert

A map and a list, which is what the GENDER Platform gives you when you click through to its experts. Reproduced rather than improved, so the pattern can be judged as it actually behaves.

Two things are deliberately faithful and one is a gap. Faithful: there is no way to search or filter these people, exactly as on the model, which was the specific criticism raised on 10/08. A gap: the map has no pins, because no location data exists for these six. Placing them anywhere would be invention.
No locations plotted. The six people below have no recorded country, so nothing can honestly be placed on this map.

Outline is indicative only and is not a geographic projection. It stands in for the interactive map the model uses.

Six people

The use-case champions already published on the current prototype.

This is not a directory. The centre-by-centre list of CGIAR climate expertise does not exist yet, and no process for keeping it current or for letting anyone opt out has been agreed. Six names is the honest current size of this function.
