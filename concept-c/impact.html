Impact

What this programme is for, and how far the evidence goes

Impact was one of the five top-level headings proposed in the Lexicon session. This page is a first attempt at filling it. It shows the published commitments, then it shows what the catalogue behind this site can currently evidence, and it does not close the gap between them with adjectives.

Evidence behind them

What the catalogue currently holds against each target

Counts are of items catalogued on this prototype, not of programme results. They say how much evidence this site can point at, which is a different question from how much work has been done.

What this page cannot yet do. An impact section that a donor would take seriously needs results, not counts of documents: farmers reached, finance mobilised, emissions avoided, each with a method and a date. None of that is in the catalogue behind this prototype, and inventing it here would make the site worse than having no impact page at all. Filling this section properly is a data question for the programme, not a design question for the site.

Honest accounting

Three things a reader should know before quoting this page

One

Counts are of catalogue entries

Every number on this page counts items recorded on this prototype. It is a measure of what has been catalogued, and nothing more. It is not a measure of activity, spend or reach.

Two

The catalogue is a sample

The entries were assembled to test a design, not to be complete. Absence from this site is not evidence that work does not exist.

Three

Nothing here is cleared

This is a feedback prototype. No figure, framing or claim on it has been through CGIAR communications, and none of it should be reused in a proposal or a brief.
