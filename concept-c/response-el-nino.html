El Niño: the situation and CGIAR's response, country by country

Section 3 of the climate.cgiar.org wishlist asks for one page that gives a clear overview of the El Niño event and what CGIAR is doing about it, a map where clicking a country opens a short summary, filters by country, issue and type of intervention, a printable view, visible dates and sources, and a template that can be reused for the next crisis or campaign. This page is that template, built on 17/09/2026 with the content the prototype can stand behind.

Overview

What this page can and cannot say yet

No CGIAR-wide statement of the El Niño situation is loaded. A cross-CGIAR El Niño Task Team is collecting Centre inventories. The only inventory available to this prototype is IITA's own submission of 21/08/2026, which is internal, covers one Centre, and names eleven African countries. Nigeria is the one country with enough in that inventory to fill the template, so it is the only summary shown. The other ten are shaded as mentioned, with no summary behind them. Nothing here describes the forecast itself, because no forecast source has been read into the site.
Filters are mock controls. The wishlist asks to keep the first set short; these four issues and four types are IITA's own grouping.

Reuse

The same page for the next crisis

The wishlist asks that this be a template, not a one-off. Everything above is driven by one list of country records with the eight fields in the panel, so a heat, flood or locust response is the same page with a different list and a different title. What the template does not solve is who keeps the list current, which was the objection to country pages on Where We Work and applies here with more force, because a response page that is a month out of date is worse than none.
