What this site is, and how to reach the programme

The programme

The CGIAR Climate Action Science Program, 2025 to 2030, describes its aim on cgiar.org as follows:

“We aim to drive science, innovation, and collaboration to transform food, land, and water systems” for a climate-resilient, net-zero and equitable future.

“Through more resilient, low-emissions farms, landscapes, and aquatic systems, we will advance research across agri-food systems benefiting 16 million vulnerable small-scale producers” and value chain actors across 20 countries.

Quoted from the Climate Action programme page on cgiar.org, read 02/11/2026. Words outside the quotation marks paraphrase the surrounding text on that page.

The five areas of work

This site

A curated front door, not another repository. It holds 37 catalogued items across datasets, publications, tools and methods, innovation catalogues, and funding calls and events, and links each to where it lives: the publisher’s own page, CGSpace, Gardian, or the Climate Data Hub for the data itself.

Every number on the site is computed from the catalogue when the page loads rather than typed in, and every metadata field was read from the provider or carried over from a checked record. Where a provider has not published a field, it is left empty.
