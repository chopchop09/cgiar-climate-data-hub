Flagship climate assets

Six tools worth knowing about

Not a complete list, and not meant to be. These are entry points: what each one is, what people come to it for, and a link out to the people who run it.

CGIAR

African Agriculture Adaptation Atlas

Spatial climate risk, adaptation options and food security across Africa, at country and sub-national level.

Come here when you need to see which adaptation options have evidence behind them for a given place in Africa.

Open the atlas ↗ Africa · web maps
CIMMYT-BISA

South Asia Climate Adaptation Atlas (ACASA)

Climate hazards, vulnerability and expert-validated adaptation options for 15 crops and six livestock species, at roughly 25 square kilometres.

Come here when you are working in South Asia and need hazard and adaptation layers at sub-district resolution.

Open ACASA ↗ South Asia · web maps
CGIAR

AgMRV

Monitoring, reporting and verification for agricultural greenhouse gas emissions, built on IPCC-aligned methodology.

Come here when a project needs to measure or report emissions and you want a method that will survive scrutiny.

Open AgMRV ↗ Global · web tools
CGIAR

AgWise

Location-specific climate-smart agronomic recommendations, with API and GIS integration.

Come here when you are building an advisory service and need agronomy that varies by location rather than one national recommendation.

Open AgWise ↗ Africa · API, GIS
CGIAR

Climate Security Observatory

Evidence base on the climate, conflict and food security nexus, with country profiles.

Come here when a proposal or a briefing has to say something defensible about climate and conflict.

Open the observatory ↗ Global · country profiles
TAAT, with CGIAR

TAAT e-catalogues

Proven agricultural technologies for scaling across Africa, organised by commodity and by value chain stage.

Come here when you need technologies that are already proven at scale rather than promising in trial.

Open the catalogues ↗ Africa · web catalogue
Six is a choice, and it is arguable. These were picked from the catalogue on this prototype as a worked example of the tile display, not as an agreed flagship set. Which assets are flagship, and who decides, is still open. The rule proposed so far is on the About page.
What is deliberately not here. The full record lives in CGSpace and Gardian. This site links to them rather than reproducing them, because a partial copy of a repository is worse than the repository.
