The five areas of work, and the themes within them

The five areas are the programme’s published structure, as on cgiar.org. The themes beneath each are an informal grouping built from the tags on the catalogued items, placed under the area where most of their items sit. Counts are matched from the catalogue at page load.

The data itself

Looking for the datasets, notebooks and tutorials?

The Climate Data Hub is the data piece: catalogued climate records with their metadata, runnable notebooks, and tutorials for getting data into Python, R or a desktop GIS. This site points to it rather than copying it.
