Ten themes

Browse by theme

The GENDER Platform organises everything under ten named research themes with sub-topics listed beneath each. This is that structure, with climate themes built from the tags already in the catalogue.

See all ten themes in full →

These ten labels are an editorial grouping, not a CGIAR taxonomy. No climate-terms taxonomy exists yet. They were built from the tags on the 44 catalogued items, exactly as the three categories on the current site were, and they should be argued with.
