Alternative spine

The ten editorial themesD2

Built from the tags on catalogued items, mirroring the ten research themes on gender.cgiar.org. Counts are computed by substring match, so any of them can be reproduced by searching for the term.

These ten labels are an editorial grouping, not a CGIAR taxonomy. No climate-terms taxonomy exists. An item can sit under more than one. The five areas of work on the home page are the published structure and are externally verifiable. Decision D2 picks one spine and retires the other.
