News, funding and events

Split into three, because the review of 11/08/2026 asked for funding calls to be kept separate from publications rather than folded into one feed.

Who keeps this current, and how often. Not yet decided. The review of 13/08/2026 put it plainly: “if it falls out of date it will look abandoned and people will stop looking.” This section carries the highest maintenance load on the site and is the one place a named owner and a review cadence have to exist before launch. Every entry below was checked when the catalogue was built on 10/08/2026 and has not been re-checked since.
Not built, and deliberately so. A newsletter sign-up, theme-specific mailing lists, a like-to-connect action on event cards and automated posting to social platforms were all proposed on the board. None appears here, because each needs a mailing tool, a cost line and an owner before it is a design question. Listing them as proposed is more honest than mocking up a form that sends nowhere.
