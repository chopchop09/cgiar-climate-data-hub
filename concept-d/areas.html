The spine

Areas of work, with use cases nested inside

The five areas of work are published on cgiar.org, so this structure can be checked against a source outside this site. Under each sits every use case recorded against it, its state, who champions it, and the catalogue items it draws on. 0 use cases are recorded in total.

Why these five and not the ten themes. Concept C organises everything under ten themes built from the tags on the catalogued items. The review of 11/08/2026 said those themes “overlap a lot, need more diffierentiation”, and unlike the five areas of work they cannot be verified against anything published. So the five carry the structure here, and the ten survive as an optional filter on the resources page, labelled there as an editorial grouping.
