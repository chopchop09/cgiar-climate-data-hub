What CGIAR knows about climate, in one place

Publications, datasets, methods and manuals, innovation catalogues, experts, news and events. Everything here links to the source that holds it. The Hub curates and points; it does not re-host and it does not replace the platforms it links to.

The bet this concept makes

That the three layers should stay separate, because they have different costs, different owners and different failure modes.

  • Tier 1 is cheap, useful on its own, and shippable this year
  • Tier 2 is a modest addition once tier 1 exists
  • Tier 3 is expensive and depends on data nobody has assembled yet
  • Any tier can be dropped without breaking the one below it

If this is right, the front page should feel plain, and that plainness should read as a feature rather than a fault. Compare against Concept B before deciding.

The feedback described three layers stacked on each other. Concept A keeps them genuinely separate, so each can be built, judged and paid for on its own. You are on tier 1.

Going further

The two doors out of tier 1

Concept A's central bet: these are separate places, not sections of this page. A visitor who only needs a link never meets them, and each can be dropped or deferred without touching tier 1.

Tier 2

Query the collections

What is CGIAR doing on drought, in East Africa, on the mitigation side? Tier 1 makes you browse six collections to answer that. Tier 2 answers it in one query, across all of them at once.

  • One keyword field, plus filters for collection, adaptation or mitigation, and geography
  • Searches every item on this prototype, not just one collection
  • Works now, on this prototype's own content only

Tier 3

Analyse, and build country profiles

The layer that makes something new: a two to four page country brief, portfolio analytics, evidence assembled rather than listed. Shown here as structure with the data layers named and no figures populated, because no country figures have been compiled.

  • The profile template, section by section, with the datasets that would fill each one
  • Portfolio analytics computed from the eight projects, not typed in by hand
  • Deliberately empty where the evidence does not exist yet
